## My tasks

**My tasks** lists everything assigned to you and still to do, across all pipes — grouped by state, by pipe or by
priority, and filtered by text, label and priority.

## Next action

**Next action** follows Getting Things Done:

1. **Capture** an idea into your Personal pipe, in one line.
2. **Finish** what is in progress: each task in progress shows its next sub-task.
3. **Start** the suggested task — late or due soon first, then ready, already begun or waiting for long. A task still
   waiting for other tasks is never suggested.

Beyond three tasks in progress, it suggests finishing one before starting another.

## Calendar

The **Calendar** page shows tasks with a due date, by **month** or **week**: your own tasks, or everything you see,
filtered by space, finished tasks on demand. Overdue tasks are marked.

**Drag a task to another day to change its due date** — if you may change it (members of its space).

## Calendar subscription

**Calendar** in the account menu gives a personal link to subscribe to your tasks in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar
or any calendar app. Each task is an all-day event with a link back to it, and open tasks carry reminders five days
before, the day before and on the due date. The link can be renewed if it leaks.
