## Live in the app

A task sent to you, refused, a mention or a task no longer blocked shows up at once, with a notification. The number
of waiting tasks shows in the tab title. After the server is updated, open pages reload themselves when nothing is
being edited.

## By e-mail

With e-mail set up on the instance:

| Notification | Who gets it | By default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A task arrives in an incoming | the person, or the members of the team | at once |
| A task becomes yours | its new owner | at once |
| A task you sent is refused | the sender, with the reason | at once |
| A comment | the task's author and owner | daily digest |
| A mention | the person mentioned | at once, unless they are in the app |
| A task is no longer blocked | its author and owner | at once, unless they are in the app |
| Due dates | the owner: overdue tasks, and those due today and tomorrow | in the morning, at 8:00 |

In **Settings**, choose for each kind *at once*, *daily digest* or *off*, and the hour of the morning reminder.

- E-mails sent at once wait two minutes, to gather what follows; a task sends at most one e-mail every 15 minutes.
- Nothing is sent for what you do yourself.
- Confidential and team-only tasks are only named by their key: their content stays in the app.
- Every e-mail has an unsubscribe link.

## On your devices

TasksPipes can be **installed** as an app, on a phone's home screen or from the browser's address bar. In
**Settings**, turn on **Notifications on this device**: you then get a notification for what you chose to get *at
once*, while TasksPipes is not open. A click opens the task. Signing out turns it off for that device.
