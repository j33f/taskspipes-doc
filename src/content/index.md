TasksPipes is an open-source, self-hosted task manager built around [Outline](https://www.getoutline.com). It gives
your workspace kanban **pipes** with typed tasks, **incoming buckets** to hand work to people and teams, and a calm
**next action** for everyone.

## Start here

- **New to TasksPipes?** Read [Getting started](/guide/getting-started), then [Pipes](/guide/pipes) and
  [Tasks](/guide/tasks).
- **Handing work over?** See [Incoming buckets](/guide/incoming).
- **Connecting other tools?** See [GitHub and GitLab](/integrations/github-gitlab), [Webhooks and chat](/integrations/webhooks)
  and the [public API](/integrations/api).
- **Running your own instance?** Follow the [installation guide](/self-hosting/installation).

## The main ideas

| Concept | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Space** | Where pipes and tasks live: everyone has a personal space, and teams have team spaces. |
| **Pipe** | A kanban board with a purpose (sales, course design, software…). Its type sets its columns and task types. |
| **Task** | A card in a pipe. Its type gives it fields; it has an owner, a due date, sub-tasks, comments and a full history. |
| **Incoming** | The inbox of a person or a team: tasks sent by others wait there to be accepted or refused. |
| **Next action** | A Getting Things Done view: finish what is in progress, then start what matters most. |

> TasksPipes works best with Outline, but does not need it: people can also sign in with e-mail links or with your
> company's single sign-on.
