Every person and every team has an **incoming bucket**: the place where tasks sent by others arrive. Your own incoming
is in the sidebar, with the number of tasks waiting.

## Sending a task

- From a task's page, **Send to…** a person or a team, with an optional message and, for a team, its owner there.
- Or create a task straight into someone's incoming (*Send a task* on their page).

A task sent to an incoming can have the plain *Task* type, or a type offered by the recipient's pipes that you can
see: the **open** pipes of someone else's space (their private pipes never show through), or every pipe of your own
space or team.

## Accepting or refusing

A member of the receiving space:

- **accepts** the task into one of its pipes and columns — and can turn it into a type that pipe offers;
- or **refuses** it with a reason: it goes back to the sender's incoming, and the sender is told.

Recipients are told at once in the app, and by e-mail or push notification according to their
[settings](/guide/notifications).

## Confidential tasks

Tasks of confidential and team-only pipes cannot be sent out of their space: their content stays where it belongs.
