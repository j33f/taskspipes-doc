## Signing in

The sign-in page offers the ways your instance allows:

- **With Outline** — type the address of your Outline server (it is remembered in your browser, and you can add
  others), then continue with Outline (OAuth) or paste a personal Outline API key. Everyone in your Outline workspace
  is imported at your first sign-in, so you can send them tasks right away.
- **With your e-mail address** — create an account with your address and a display name; a link sent by e-mail signs
  you in. You can also be invited by someone already on TasksPipes.
- **With your company's sign-in** — *Continue with …*, when your administrator has set up OpenID Connect.

## Finding your way

The sidebar holds everything:

- **Search** (or **Ctrl + K** / **⌘ + K**) finds any task you can see;
- **My dashboard**, **My tasks**, **Next action** and **Calendar** gather your work across all pipes;
- your **Incoming** shows tasks waiting for you, with a count;
- your **pipes**, **pinned** pipes, **teams** and **people** follow;
- your name, at the bottom, opens the account menu: settings, invitations, calendar link, about, sign out.

## Your first pipe

Every personal space starts with a **Personal** pipe for general tasks. To create another one, open a space (your
own, or a team's) and click **New pipe**: give it a name, choose a **type** from the catalog — its columns and task
types come with it — and decide whether it is private or **open** (others can add tasks to it).

## Your first task

On a pipe's board, click **New task** (or **Add** at the bottom of a column). Give it a title, pick a task type if the
pipe offers several, fill in its fields, and save. Then drag it from column to column as work moves on.

To hand a task to someone, use **Send to…**: it lands in their [incoming](/guide/incoming).
