---
title: Search
description: Search every pipe and incoming bucket you can see with Ctrl + K or ⌘ + K: find a task by the words of its title, description, fields, comments or its key.
---

**Search** at the top of the sidebar, or **Ctrl + K** (**⌘ + K** on a Mac) anywhere, finds tasks in every pipe and
incoming bucket you can see:

- by **title**, **description**, **sub-tasks**, **field values**, the title of the **linked Outline document** and
  **comments**, or by **number** (`TP-123`, `tp123` or `123`);
- results come as you type: each word is the start of a word to find, all of them must match, accents do not matter,
  and words are matched in your language (*inscription* also finds *inscriptions*);
- each result shows its key, title, owner and place, and an excerpt with the words found highlighted;
- filters: status (or *not finished*), owner, task type, label and priority; ↑ ↓ and Enter open a task.

A search only finds what you could open in the app. **Confidential** and **team-only** tasks show no excerpt: their
content stays on their page.
